# Simplified Technical English review

**Current status: Not verified against the complete official ASD-STE100 Issue 9 rules and dictionary.**

1. Obtain the authorized ASD-STE100 Issue 9 reference.
2. Identify the documents to review.
3. Compare vocabulary and sentence structure with the official rules.
4. Review the [project-specific technical terminology](terminology.md).
5. Correct identified violations while preserving correct software terminology.
6. Record the reviewer, reference issue, date, exceptions, and results.
7. Do not claim full compliance unless a review supports that claim.

The automated checker does **not** perform this full review.
